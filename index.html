About

A Pennsylvania company with a long view.

Winter Ridge Ventures LLC is the parent company for a growing set of independent projects. Some products operate under their own names as trade names of the company, so each one can have its own identity while sharing the same standards.

We favor simple tools, privacy by design, and software that earns its place on your device. We'd rather ship one thing that works well than ten that almost do.
