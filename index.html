About

A Pittsburgh company with a long view.

Winter Ridge Ventures LLC is a Pennsylvania limited liability company based in Pittsburgh, and the parent company for a growing set of independent projects. Some products operate under their own names as trade names of the company, so each one can have its own identity while sharing the same standards.

Security and privacy are the deciding factors in every design choice we make. If a feature would weaken either one, we don't build it. Your data stays yours, protected by strong encryption and kept on your own device wherever possible.

We favor simple tools and software that earns its place on your device. We'd rather ship one thing that works well than ten that almost do.
